Join

The Spatiotemporal Spectroscopic Imaging Lab explores the intricate dynamics of complex liquids using cutting-edge techniques and interdisciplinary approaches. Whether you're a student, researcher, or collaborator, we invite you to be part of our journey to push the boundaries of molecular science. We are looking for postdoctoral researchers, graduate students, and research assistants to join our dynamic team. Our lab fosters a culture of innovation, collaboration, and personal growth. Enrollment Plan

 
Postdoctoral Fellow
We are looking for postdoctoral researchers in the following research fields:
1. PhD in Chemistry, Physics, Materials Science, or a related field; research background in high-resolution near-field imaging and/or time-resolved spectroscopy techniques are preferred.  
2. PhD in Chemistry, Physics, Materials Science, or a related field; research background in high-resolution near-field imaging and/or time-resolved spectroscopy techniques are preferred.  
3. PhD in Chemistry, Physics, Materials Science, or a related field; research background in high-resolution near-field imaging and/or time-resolved spectroscopy techniques are preferred.  
 
Research Assistant
We are looking for research assistants in the following directions:
1. Optics and physical chemistry.  
2. Computational Chemistry and Analytical Chemistry.  
3. Biochemistry and Biomedical Engineering.
